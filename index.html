Our Love Story

"My bounty is as boundless as the sea, my love as deep..."

It began in a city of old rivalry, where two worlds were never meant to collide. A single shared glance across a crowded room silenced the chaos around us. In that quiet instant, history faded, and our future was instantly rewritten.

No longer bound by the shadows of yesterday, we chose a path of fierce light, unwavering devotion, and endless laughter. Together, we have built a sanctuary out of starlight—and now, we invite you to celebrate the triumph of a love destined to endure.
